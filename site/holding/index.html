The open knowledge graph of AI models.

What each model can do, what it costs, where it runs, and how it scores on every benchmark we can find. Free, open source, and built for anyone who has to choose a model and be right.

"What's the best model I can actually run on my hardware, for my use case, that my organization is allowed to use?"
That question, answered from evidence, and kept current as the models change underneath you.

01The graph

Every model as a node, linked to its provider, its lineage, its competitors, the platforms that serve it, the benchmarks that measured it, its capabilities and its licence. Eight views, one graph, explorable in 3D.

02The downselect

Tell it your use case, where you can host, what hardware you have, from a Raspberry Pi to a B200, and the rules you live under: open weights, origin, cost, context. It filters, scores, ranks, and shows its reasons.

03The CLI

The same graph from your terminal, for scripts and for the tools that build with you.

modelspec rank --use-case coding --top 10
modelspec compare anthropic/claude-opus-4-6 openai/o3
modelspec hardware macbook_air_m4_24gb
modelspec gaps --top 20
