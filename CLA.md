## Why this exists

ModelSpec is published under two licences: MIT for the code, and Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 for the curated corpora in `models/` and `benchmarks/`.

A licence can only be changed with the agreement of everyone who holds copyright in the work. Without an agreement like this one, the licence a project launches with is effectively the licence it keeps for ever, because tracking down every past contributor is impractical. This agreement keeps the option open, so that the project can, for example, offer a commercial licence to an organisation that cannot accept share-alike terms, without asking each contributor for permission.

It does not take your copyright. It does not stop you using your own contribution however you like, anywhere else.

## How to agree

Add a `Signed-off-by` line to each commit, using `git commit -s`:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
```

That line means you have read this agreement and agree to it for that contribution. Use your real name and an address you can be reached at.

For a substantial or ongoing contribution, the Project may also ask you to confirm agreement in writing.
