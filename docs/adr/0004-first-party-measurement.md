# ModelSpec measures, not only catalogues

Until 2026-09-24 ModelSpec's policy was to catalogue other publishers' numbers and never produce its own, which is why a latency programme was cancelled. We reversed that on 2026-09-24. The deepest decisions need evidence nobody else publishes: speed per offering, benchmark results that labs leave out, and whether a routed task actually succeeded. We produce it in three steps, each approved by Jamie before any spend:
1. passive outcome records from agents acting on decisions, starting with DPF, at no added cost;
2. active speed probes on premier offerings;
3. runs of open benchmarks.

Every first-party number carries `measured_by: ModelSpec` or the outcome protocol, a published method, its sample size and the kind of workload. The DPF relationship is disclosed wherever DPF-derived evidence appears.
