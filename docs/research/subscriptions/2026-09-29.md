# Subscription plans, research round 2 (MODEL-201)

Read 2026-09-29. Every fact below comes from the provider's own pricing, plan or
help page, read that day. Plan records: `offerings/subscriptions/*.yaml`,
written by `scripts/model_201_collect.py`. Retained copies live outside git in
the `CopyStore` (`~/.cache/modelspec/sources`); the collector pins each source
to the copy that was read.

Quotes are verbatim, including the providers' spelling and punctuation. A table
cell such as `Opus | No | Yes | Yes | Yes` is the page's own row, as the
normaliser renders it. Where a page draws a cell as an icon, the cell is given
as the icon's `aria-label` from the retained HTML and marked **(aria-label)**.

### Verification

The collector filed 106 claims: 85 new or changed known values and 21 checked
not-disclosed values (the other known values are MODEL-173's, already
verified). `modelspec verify` confirmed 94 of them, including every price,
billing period and models-covered value. Twelve verbatim strings stay
quarantined: the access wording for ChatGPT
Go, Grok Business, Mistral Pro, the three Z.ai plans, the three MiniMax plans and
Alibaba's Coding Plan, and the allowance wording for ChatGPT Business Premium
and Mistral Pro. Each sits on a page that lists several plans, where no reader
can yet tie the sentence to one plan. Phase 2 replaces these strings with
`surfaces` and `allowance.*` facts.

Readers: the deterministic `SubscriptionPageExtractor`, extended in this PR for
the new layouts (plan cards, tier lines, plan-column and plan-row tables,
ChatGPT's per-plan model rows, "All plans support …", "### Models" lists, and
"What is the <plan>?" articles), and Mistral Large, which is independent of the
Claude collector. Mistral confirmed the first Google prices but misread most
plan pages. It quoted two non-adjacent lines as one sentence, and a reply
cached for one plan was reused for its siblings on the same page (the cache
key omits the plan names).

Surfaces and verbatim coverage wording have no facet in the MODEL-173 shape.
They are recorded here, per plan, and move into MODEL-200's `surfaces`,
`families_covered` and `coverage_quote` facets in phase 2.

### Reuse terms

ModelSpec stores facts (a price, a billing period, a plan name, which models a
plan lists) and short verbatim quotes as evidence, each attributed to its URL.
Full page copies are retained locally for re-verification and are not
committed or published. No provider's site terms were reviewed page by page in
this round; nothing here reproduces more than a table row or a sentence.
No source barred by `tests/test_removed_sources.py` was used, and third-party
pricing blogs found by search (for Kimi) were not used as sources.

### Fetching

Plain HTTP read most pages. OpenAI's help centre answered a browser user agent.
Seven pages render their content client-side and were read as Firecrawl
markdown: `x.ai/pricing` (a second copy), `x.ai/grok/business`,
`grok.com/supergrok`, `help.x.com/en/using-x/x-premium`, `z.ai/subscribe`,
`perplexity.ai/pro`, and the Perplexity Pro help article. That is 7 scrapes,
far under the 150-credit cap; the shared account balance was 1,988 at the start
and 1,844 afterwards, the difference being mostly other sessions.
